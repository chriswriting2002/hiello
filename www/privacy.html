Hiello Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Hiello lets you share photos and short videos with a small, private group of people you choose, called your Circle. This page explains, in plain English, what Hiello collects, why, and what you can do about it. Hiello is made by Christopher Guzman (“we”).

The short version

  • Only the people in your Circle can see what you send.
  • There are no ads. We don’t sell your information, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account, and everything you’ve sent, at any time in the app.

What we collect

  • Your name, the one you type in, so your Circle knows who sent each hi.
  • What you share: photos, videos, captions, notes, replies, hearts, and answers like “I’ll be there.”
  • Whether a hi was seen, so the sender can see a ✓.
  • A notification token from Apple, if you allow notifications. It lets us send your phone a ding when someone in your Circle sends something.
  • Join attempts: when someone types a Circle code, we record the attempt and the internet (IP) address it came from, only to block people who keep guessing codes.

We don’t ask for your email, phone number, contacts or location, and we don’t use advertising or analytics trackers.

Camera and photo library

Hiello uses your camera only when you take a photo or video to send, and your photo library only for the pictures you pick. Nothing else is uploaded.

Who can see your photos

Only members of your Circle. Anyone who has a valid Circle code can join your Circle, so share codes and QR codes only with people you trust. Codes expire after 7 days.

Where it’s stored

Your Circle’s photos and messages are stored with our cloud provider, Supabase, on servers in the United States, in private storage that only your Circle can reach through the app. Notifications are delivered through Apple’s Push Notification service. The Hiello widget keeps small copies of recent photos on your own phone so it can show them on your home screen.

How long we keep it

We keep what you send until you delete it.

  • Leave a Circle: your phone stops receiving, and what you already sent stays for the others.
  • Delete your account (Profile › Delete my account): we delete your name, every hi you sent, your replies and notes, and the photo and video files. If you’re the last person in the Circle, the whole Circle is deleted.

Copies already saved to someone else’s phone, for example a screenshot, are outside our control.

Children

Hiello isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. Parents may share photos of their own children with their Circle.

Your choices and rights

You can turn notifications off in iPhone Settings, change your name in the app, leave a Circle, or delete your account at any time. Depending on where you live, for example in California or the EU, you may have the right to know what we hold about you and to ask us to delete it. Deleting your account in the app does that. You can also contact us. We don’t sell or “share” personal information as California law defines those terms.

Security

We use private storage, secret Circle keys, and checks on every request so that only your Circle can reach your photos. No system is perfect, so please only share what you’re comfortable sharing with your Circle.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top and, for big changes, let you know in the app.
